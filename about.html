About Mehedi Tanim

Also known as Mehedi Hasan Tanim · Regensburg, Germany

Background

I am Mehedi Tanim, an Electrical & Electronics Engineering graduate from Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University (HSTU), Bangladesh. I am pursuing a master's degree in Electrical & Microsystems Engineering at OTH Regensburg, Germany.

My interests span semiconductor devices, photovoltaics, microsystems, electronic circuits and electrical engineering research. I have used SCAPS-1D and MATLAB in simulation-based investigations of lead-free perovskite solar cells.

Engineering experience

I worked as an Electrical Engineer at Quality Feeds Limited in Dhaka in 2025 and completed engineering training at Bangladesh Power Development Board (BPDB) in 2024.
