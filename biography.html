Mehedi Tanim

Mehedi Hasan Tanim · Electrical Engineer & Semiconductor Researcher · Regensburg, Germany

Biography

Mehedi Tanim (also known as Mehedi Hasan Tanim) is an electrical engineer pursuing a master's degree in Electrical & Microsystems Engineering at OTH Regensburg, Germany. He completed his bachelor's degree in Electrical & Electronics Engineering at Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University (HSTU), Bangladesh.

His technical interests include semiconductor devices, photovoltaics, microsystems, electronic circuits and simulation-based device research, including lead-free perovskite solar cells. His research tools include SCAPS-1D and MATLAB.

His engineering experience includes working as an Electrical Engineer at Quality Feeds Limited, Dhaka (January–July 2025), and an engineering internship at Bangladesh Power Development Board (BPDB) (May–June 2024).

Research interests

His research explores lead-free photovoltaic materials, transport-layer selection and solar-cell device architectures. Topics represented in his portfolio include Cs₂TiBr₆ and K₂TiI₆ solar cells, kesterite hole transport layers, and perovskite/kesterite dual-absorber structures.
